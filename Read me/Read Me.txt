4. Database / Dataset
Since this is a security-focused logic model, the "Database" is implemented as an In-Memory Data Structure to demonstrate the schema without requiring a heavy SQL installation.

Database Structure:

DB_PATIENTS: Master registry of authorized patients.

DB_DOCTORS: Master registry of authorized medical practitioners.

db_medical_records: The "Secure Vault" containing encrypted medical data.

db_insurance_ledgers: Financial records for balance tracking.
